# Captions — back-and-forth sequences after large saccades

A back-and-forth sequence is a **large** unique gaze event (≥ 7.0°) followed within **150 ms** by **≥2 smaller** detected saccades, with at least one counter-directed relative to the primary (circular difference > 90°) and at least one consecutive pair of extras that reverse relative to each other.

**back_and_forth_overall.pdf.** Frequency among large saccades: **18.1%** (799/4416).

**back_and_forth_by_head.pdf.** Same sequences, split by `lizMov` head annotation on the primary saccade. Stationary: **11.3%** (n=1419). Moving: **21.3%** (n=2997). Fisher p=7.64e-17.
